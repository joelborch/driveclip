DriveClip GitHub
Open source Chrome extension

Record a tab. It lands in your Drive.

DriveClip records your tab or your whole screen and streams it into your own Google Drive as it goes, so the share link is ready the second you stop. No accounts to make, no servers of ours in between.

Get it on GitHub Read the setup guide

Not on the Chrome Web Store yet. Clone the repo, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and load the extension/ folder unpacked.

How a recording travelsSource → your Drive → a link
Source

A tab, a window, or the screen

Pick what to capture and whether the mic is on. Recording starts from the popup.

Destination

Your Google Drive, live

Chunks upload while you record, into one folder the extension creates. It only ever touches files it made itself.

Output

A link you can send

Stopping hands you a viewer link. Whoever opens it watches the clip, nothing else.
